Privacy policy

Effective 24 October 2026. Applies to Inkfin 0.1.2 and this website. Publisher: Priyanshu Maurya.

Your documents stay on your computer

Inkfin is a read-only local Markdown viewer. The app reads documents you choose and supported local images referenced by those documents. It watches the open document for changes so the preview can refresh. It does not upload document contents or paths to the publisher, and it does not edit your source documents.

Local preferences

The app saves your theme and zoom choices locally. It does not maintain a recent-document list or a cloud account. These settings are not sent to the publisher. Uninstalling a Store package and removing the app's data removes its local settings; standalone installations may retain settings in the Windows user profile.

No app analytics or advertising

Inkfin does not include developer-operated analytics, advertising, accounts or telemetry. Remote images in Markdown are blocked. When you choose an HTTP or HTTPS link in a document, the app opens it in your default browser; the destination website's privacy policy then applies.

Windows, Microsoft Store and WebView2

Windows, Microsoft Store and Microsoft Edge WebView2 are separate Microsoft services or components. Their diagnostics, updates, installation records and privacy settings are governed by Microsoft. Inkfin's local processing statement does not describe or override those services.

This website

Website hosting and delivery services process connection information such as IP addresses, request URLs and browser headers to deliver and secure pages. The home page requests fonts from Google Fonts, which receives connection information when those resources load. The privacy and support pages use system fonts. This website's source does not include an analytics or advertising SDK.

Support requests

If you submit an issue on GitHub, GitHub and the publisher can see the information you choose to share. Public issues are visible to other people. Do not include private Markdown documents, passwords or sensitive file paths. Support information is used to investigate and respond to the issue; GitHub's own retention and privacy practices also apply.

Questions and changes

Use the support page to contact the publisher or ask about privacy. This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change.
